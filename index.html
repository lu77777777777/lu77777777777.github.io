> Research_

What I research

My research is about learning from more than one source: fusing complementary modalities into one representation, and transferring knowledge from one scene or domain to another. My PhD at UTS developed these ideas for hyperspectral remote sensing. Today I apply the same rigour to LLM and agent systems in industry.

Transfer learning · Domain adaptation · Knowledge distillation

Cross-scene knowledge transfer

Carrying what a model learns on one hyperspectral scene over to another, despite different sensors and mismatched label sets: aligning spectra and gradients while keeping the target-specific patterns a source model would miss.

IEEE TGRS (in review) · Remote Sensing

Multimodal · Information fusion

Multimodal information fusion

Combining complementary data into one representation: hyperspectral imagery with LiDAR elevation, and DNA, RNA and protein profiles measured in the same cell.

Neurocomputing · Briefings in Bioinformatics

Information retrieval · Computer vision

Image retrieval and feature encoding

Fusing multi-level CNN features into compact codes, with dimensionality reduction that keeps retrieval accurate while the index stays small.

Multimedia Tools and Applications · Turkish J. EECS

Applied AI

Task-specific AI with guardrails

In industry: AI models built for specific tasks in legal and fintech, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) grounded in domain documents, guardrails that keep LLM outputs safe and compliant, evaluation designed before the system, and multi-agent workflows that hold up outside the demo.
